The three problems

parser: day and month swapped. The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 describes. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first, as told, and the form reads the date day first. The dates come out swapped too, but the parser fix does not touch them: the form hint had to change. Only reports sent through the booking-v3 form have this cause. Won’t fix: booking-v3 is retired.

shift: a date moved by one day. Some reports are not a swap at all. The date is one day early or late: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk marked these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). A swap cannot move a date by one day, so these reports have a cause of their own, probably time zones. Nobody has looked yet.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line. All three problems produce subjects like “Wrong date again”, so they landed in one ticket. Intake’s tag does not help either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the table shows how often it guessed a different cause.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can hold several bugs with the same symptom. Count by cause, not by subject.
  • Check the outliers. Five reports that do not fit are a bug, not noise.
